Ted Kwartler

Head of Advanced AI & Responsible AI for the Americas at Accenture; Instructor at Harvard Extension School

Ted Kwartler is a Managing Director at Accenture, where he heads Advanced AI and Responsible AI for the Americas, bringing data science, generative AI, and large language models into responsible practice across industries. He is also an instructor at Harvard Extension School, teaching data mining and business analytics, and the author of Text Mining in Practice with R, Sports Analytics in Practice with R, and Applied Sport Business Analytics. Earlier he was field CTO for generative AI and large language models at DataRobot, with prior roles at Amazon and Liberty Mutual, and he holds an MBA from the University of Notre Dame. He builds open AI projects, including Waiback, an archive of how every language model answers the same fixed questions, preserved over time.
