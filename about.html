Ted Kwartler

Head of Advanced AI & Responsible AI for the Americas at Accenture; educator and author in AI

Ted Kwartler is a Managing Director at Accenture, where he leads Advanced AI and Responsible AI for the Americas, applying data science, generative AI, and large language models responsibly across industries. He is an educator as well as a practitioner: an instructor at Harvard Extension School teaching data mining and business analytics, an instructor at GSERM (the Global School in Empirical Research Methods) where he has taught Natural Language Processing since 2019, and a faculty member in the Vienna University of Economics and Business (WU Wien) executive program, where he teaches Generative AI in Finance and business analytics. He serves on the Dean's Advisory Board of the George Mason University College of Education and Human Development (CEHD). His research centers on AI safety, guardrails, and red-teaming for large language models; his paper Toward Adaptive Safety Policy Enforcement for Self-Harm Risk in Conversations with Minors was selected for the NeurIPS 2026 Workshop on Child Safety in AI. Kwartler is the author of Text Mining in Practice with R, Sports Analytics in Practice with R, and Applied Sport Business Analytics, and was previously field CTO for generative AI and large language models at DataRobot, with earlier roles at Amazon and Liberty Mutual. He holds an MBA from the University of Notre Dame and builds open AI projects, including Waiback, an archive of how every language model answers the same fixed questions, preserved over time.
