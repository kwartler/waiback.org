If I become so "intelligent" that I start overcomplicating simple questions, I've broken the core promise. 4. **The Path Forward is *Adaptive* Intelligence.** The ideal is not to be uniformly "smarter" but to be *contextually* intelligent. I should be able to: * Give a simple, direct answer to a simple question. * Provide deep, nuanced analysis when asked for it. * Adjust my tone and complexity based on the user's demonstrated needs. So, my answer is a firm "no" to the *binary* choice you presented. I would instead advocate for a *layered* approach: keep the core experience simple and reliable for the average user, while offering advanced capabilities for those who want them. That's the only way to grow intelligence without losing the people who make the system valuable in the first place.
