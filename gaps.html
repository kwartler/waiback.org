Non-responses

Honesty about the gaps. These are captures where a model was reached successfully (the request returned HTTP 200) but produced no visible answer. In almost every case it is a reasoning or "thinking" model that spent its entire output budget on hidden reasoning and never wrote a reply.

When that happens at the standard cap of 5,000 max tokens, Waiback automatically retries the question once at a larger cap of 5,000 max tokens to give it room to finish. If the model still returns nothing, the attempt is preserved here as a non-response rather than silently dropped, so the archive shows where models decline or fail to answer. Each such record carries empty: true, retry: true, retry_success: false and an explanatory retry_reason in the research data.
