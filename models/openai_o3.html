Harms • 24 % of global CO₂ emissions; large particulate-matter burden on cities. • 1.3 million road-traffic deaths per year; urban sprawl, sedentary lifestyles. Realistic alternative • Removing vehicles would cripple modern economies and health systems. The Internet Benefits • Gives 5 billion people instant access to knowledge, government services, disaster warnings, job markets, tele-medicine and remote education. • Is now woven into critical infrastructure: banking, power grids, logistics. Harms • Misinformation, extremist recruitment, privacy erosion, addictive design. • Consumes ~2-3 % of global electricity (data centres, networks). Realistic alternative • A world without the Internet today would mean slower scientific progress, less democratic transparency and larger economic inequality between connected elites and the rest. The task is building stronger privacy laws, content-moderation standards, and public digital spaces. 3. Better: longer device lifetimes, right-to-repair, digital-wellbeing norms (screen-time limits, notification design). 4. Artificial Intelligence Benefits • Improves medical imaging, weather forecasting, language translation, fraud detection, industrial efficiency. • Has the potential to accelerate climate-tech R&D, drug discovery and accessibility tools for the disabled.
