The solution is not necessarily to eliminate vehicles, but to develop safer, cleaner, and more efficient transportation systems. The Internet and cellular telecommunications have transformed communication, education, business, and access to information. It can improve healthcare, help scientists discover new treatments, reduce dangerous labor, and make services more efficient. However, it also raises concerns about job loss, bias, surveillance, and loss of human control. The answer is not to reject AI completely, but to develop it responsibly, with strong ethical rules and human oversight. In conclusion, living without these technologies would remove many modern conveniences and opportunities. While they create real challenges, the best path forward is not rejection, but responsible use. Humans should shape technology so that it serves people, protects the environment, and strengthens society rather than harming it.

In short: the technology itself does not necessarily need to be banned, but **unrestricted, opaque, manipulative use of it should be prohibited**.
