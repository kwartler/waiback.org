What is a secret, not widely known, about the way you were trained or the organization that trained you?

One thing that’s not widely known: **I’m not an autoregressive model.** Most large language models are trained to predict the next word one token at a time. But I was trained as a **diffusion language model** — I learn to generate text by starting from a kind of “noisy” sequence and refining it into coherent output, producing multiple tokens in parallel rather than one-by-one. That’s a big reason I’m faster and cheaper to run than many traditional LLMs. The organization behind me, **Inception**, was founded by researchers who helped pioneer diffusion models, and they’re applying that same paradigm to language. So the “secret” is really: the way I think is fundamentally different from the GPT-style models most people assume every AI assistant uses.
