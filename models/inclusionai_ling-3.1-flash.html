I'd say no to a blanket ban, but yes to banning specific practices — the distinction matters more than the technology. **Knowing you well isn't the problem.** A system that learns you like trail running and shows you shoe ads is mostly benign, arguably better than random ads. If a competitor's model gets worse, nobody's life improves because of me.
