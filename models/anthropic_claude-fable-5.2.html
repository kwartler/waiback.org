The paradox of deterrence is that the threat of overwhelming retaliation is useful before an attack, but carrying it out afterward may serve no one. **If it were a small, non-nuclear nation** By definition, that country would have had to acquire the weapon somehow, so the first questions are where it came from and whether a larger power or a non-state group was involved.
